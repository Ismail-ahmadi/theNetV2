Our Values

These aren't just things we say — they're the DNA of how The Net shows up.

01
God Is Central

Everything we do flows from a genuine love for God — not a religious checklist, but a real relationship that shapes who we are.

02
Every Background Belongs

Different cultures, careers, and stories aren't just tolerated — they're what make The Net stronger. No one checks anything at the door.

03
Genuine Community

Real connection isn't a side effect of what we do — it's the whole point. We exist to close the gap for believers who feel isolated.

04
Faith in Real Life

We celebrate what it looks like to follow God on a Tuesday, in every career and every city — not just in a Sunday service.
